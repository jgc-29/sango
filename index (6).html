Questions

Asked often, answered plainly.

What exactly happens at a Sango Check?

One hour: blood draw (ten minutes), oral microbiome screen (fifteen), full-body mole mapping (twenty), vitals, then a clinician reviews your skin, mouth and vitals with you. Bloods are explained within the week. Everything lands in one plain-English report with three actions.

What's in the blood test?
  • Full cholesterol profile — total, HDL, LDL, non-HDL, triglycerides
  • HbA1c blood sugar · hs-CRP inflammation
  • Liver function · kidney function (eGFR) · thyroid TSH
  • Full blood count · ferritin iron · vitamin D

Check Plus adds ApoB, Lp(a), fasting insulin, hormones, B12, folate and an ECG.

Is the AI mole mapping certified?

Yes — certified medical-grade dermatology imaging. The AI never makes the final call: a clinician reviews every flag, and we arrange dermatology referrals when needed.

What is an oral microbiome screen?

A read of the bacteria and inflammation signals in your gums and saliva — measures consistently linked with cardiovascular and metabolic risk. It includes gum health scoring, salivary sampling and an oral cancer check most people have never once had.

What if you find something?

Then the visit did its job — early, when options are widest. A clinician explains it in person, arranges the referral, and writes the letter your GP or specialist needs. You're never left alone with a result.

Where are you, and how does the waitlist work?

We operate in Jersey and Guernsey. Our current appointment slots are full — email the waitlist and we'll hold your place, then contact you with dates as the next release opens. No payment is taken until you book a confirmed slot.
